# Ristretto colour curves

The colour bands fan in one after another, outermost first. A front runs along each band's curve from its left end, and the band slides in behind it, overshoots and springs back. Last, the dark dome at the bottom rises into the middle of the fan, stretching tall as it overshoots and settling with a squash.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0.3 | The outer brown band starts to sweep in; the rest follow every 0.2 s |
| 2.3 | The last band, the maroon ring around the dome, sweeps in |
| 2.55 | The dome rises from the bottom edge on a spring |
| 4.3 | Every piece is exactly in place; the frame is the wallpaper |
| 5.0 | The pattern blends into the exact still |

The wallpaper is paper strips lying on one another, two of them crossing twice, so its flat regions are not bands: cut as they are seen they have corners wherever a band dips under another. `split.py` saves each band as the whole strip it would be on its own: stacked bottom to top by which side of each edge carries the rim and shadow, each edge carried on under the bands above it (along the smooth curve joining the stretches it owns, or a reach in under the band above) and straight on past the screen's edges, in its own colour, with the shadow it casts saved as a dark fringe. The two orange regions are one band, cut in two by the lens and the red band. Drawn back in place the bands are the wallpaper but for thin rims, so from 4.3 s the scene eases the still in while nothing moves. Pieces swing about the ellipse the inner domes curve around, so each band travels roughly along its own curve; each band's leading end fades over a soft front as wide as half its travel in a frame.

Where two strips cross, a little ground can still show between them while both spring at different moments, near the crossing points.

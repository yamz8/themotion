# Catppuccin waves

A rippling signal line draws across the screen with a spark at its head, then the waves swell out of it from left to right on a spring, ring and calm, and a soft pulse of light runs along them before the picture settles.

The lines cross each other everywhere, so the ribbon is one layer, coloured per column, and the scene moves it in 4 px vertical strips.

The thin lines lose a little to h264: the rendered last frame matches the wallpaper exactly, and the encoded one ends at a mean difference of about 0.26 against the 0.3 limit. CRF 12 only gets to 0.17 at half again the size, so the pattern's CRF stays.

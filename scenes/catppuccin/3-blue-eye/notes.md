# Blue eye opens

A seed of light appears at the centre and the swirl of rays shoots out of the pupil, twisting into place. A glowing pen sweeps once around the eye, tracing the ring of petal loops as it spins down behind it. The ring closes with a pulse; the eye blinks, its pupil dilates as it opens, and it glances left and right before the picture settles.

The wallpaper is one colour of line-work on a flat background, so the split is exact: the rays inside radius 185 px (at 4K) and the petals outside it.

# Rose Pine Omarchy plants

The sun rises from behind the hill and springs into place. The plants spring up out of the ground in a wave from each edge towards the middle, whipping upright as they grow, and the sprouts pop up around them. The letters of OMARCHY drop in one by one and land with a squash, then a breeze runs through the plants from left to right before everything settles.

The plants overlap and some are softly blurred, so they don't come apart into single plants. Each cluster is one layer, grown by a warp that scales every column up from the ground and bends it by the square of its height, resampled per device pixel so neighbouring columns leave no seams. From 4.95 s the clusters are drawn unwarped.

The sky's gradient runs in steps along a 47° diagonal, so the backdrop behind the plants is rebuilt from a per-band profile across that diagonal; the ground is a profile by depth below its edge, which takes in the edge's soft rows. What the model misses is carried in from the surrounding pixels, so the backdrop has no seam when the plants are small. The letters touch, so they part by colour.

The cream paper is bright, so the fade from black runs 0.8 s to keep each frame's step under 12 levels.
